Privacy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Dockup's marketing site (this page and the handful of pages linked from it) does not collect any personal data. There's no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no server-side logging of visitors, and no contact form on this site to submit data through.

What is collected

Nothing. This is a static page served with no backend - there's no database and nothing to store a visitor's details in even if we wanted to.

If you get in touch

If you email privacy@lightmorphic.com, that email and any details you choose to include in it are handled under Lightmorphic's own privacy practice for its business correspondence: kept only as long as needed to answer you and for ordinary business record-keeping, never sold, and never shared beyond what's needed to reply.

Dockup the software

This page is about the marketing site only. Dockup itself is self-hosted software you run on your own server - once installed, it and the data it manages live entirely on your own machine, not on anything Lightmorphic operates or can see.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected by this site, there's nothing held to access, correct or delete. If you've emailed Lightmorphic directly and want that correspondence removed, ask at privacy@lightmorphic.com and it will be actioned.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email privacy@lightmorphic.com.
