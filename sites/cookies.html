This site sets no cookies and does no tracking of any kind.

No analytics script, no advertising pixel, no session cookie, no local storage used to remember or identify you. Nothing on this page phones home to any third party. You can browse it with cookies disabled entirely in your browser and nothing will behave any differently.

Lightmorphic Dockup

A self-hosted Docker Compose stack manager for home labs - every stack, its logs and its terminal in one window, with a real login and two-factor. Docker or Podman, no lock-in.

No cookies and no tracking. The only thing loaded from anywhere else is the Lightmorphic app launcher in the menu bar.
